Working Papers

Public Internet Hospitals and Physicians’ Online Dual Practice: Evidence from Third-Party Platforms in China

Ruirui Yang, Hongqiao Fu, and Duo Xu

Work in progress.

Abstract. Do physicians expand or reduce their practice on third-party platforms when their hospitals launch online services? We link hand-collected launch dates from 214 tertiary public hospitals to more than 420,000 records of physician activity on third-party platforms from 2019 to 2024. We use a staggered difference-in-differences design to study changes in participation and service intensity.
