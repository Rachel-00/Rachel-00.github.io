Working Papers

Public Internet Hospitals and Physicians’ Online Dual Practice: Evidence from Third-Party Platforms in China

Ruirui Yang, Hongqiao Fu, and Duo Xu

Abstract. We study whether physicians change their activity on third-party platforms when public hospitals in China launch their own online services. Using physician-level panel data and a difference-in-differences design, we examine changes in participation and service intensity and ask whether they vary by professional rank.
