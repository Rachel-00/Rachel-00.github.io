About Me

I am a Master of Public Health (MPH) candidate at Peking University, with a bachelor's degree from the Capital University of Economics and Business. My research focuses on health policy and systems research, with particular interest in health inequality, healthcare access, and the evaluation of public health interventions using quasi-experimental methods.

I have collaborated with researchers at MIT Sloan, the Chinese Academy of Social Sciences (CASS), the University of Amsterdam, and the Chinese University of Hong Kong. My work has been published in The Lancet (Q1, IF = 8.1) and presented at international conferences including the International Health Economics Association (iHEA) and the European Health Economics Association (EuHEA).
