Working Papers

Public Internet Hospitals and Physicians’ Online Dual Practice: Evidence from Third-Party Platforms in China

Ruirui Yang, Hongqiao Fu, and Duo Xu

Work in progress.

Abstract. Do physicians expand or reduce their practice on third-party platforms when their hospitals launch online services? We link hand-collected launch dates from 214 tertiary public hospitals to platform records for more than 70,000 physicians over 2019–2024. We use a staggered difference-in-differences design to study changes in participation and service intensity.
